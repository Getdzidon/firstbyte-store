Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed the next business day.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order has shipped, you will receive an email with your tracking number. You can use this number on the carrier's website to monitor your delivery in real time.

International Shipping

We ship to over 50 countries. Please note that international orders may be subject to customs duties and import taxes, which are the responsibility of the recipient. Delivery times may vary depending on customs processing in your country.

Shipping Restrictions

Some products cannot be shipped to certain regions due to local regulations. If your order cannot be fulfilled for your location, you will be notified at checkout.

Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package arrives damaged or goes missing, please contact us at our support page within 7 days of the expected delivery date and we will resolve it promptly.
