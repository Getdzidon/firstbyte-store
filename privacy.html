Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, shipping address, and payment details when you place an order or create an account. We also collect usage data such as pages visited and products viewed to improve your experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To send order confirmations and shipping updates
  • To respond to your enquiries and support requests
  • To improve our website and product offerings
  • To send promotional emails (you can opt out at any time)

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with trusted third-party service providers (e.g. payment processors, shipping carriers) solely to fulfil your orders. These partners are bound by confidentiality agreements.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to remember your preferences, keep items in your cart, and analyse site traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function correctly.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard SSL encryption to protect your data during transmission. Payment information is processed securely and never stored on our servers.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To make a request, contact us at our support page.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our site after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
