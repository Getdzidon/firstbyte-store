How to Start a Return

  1. Contact us via the Help Center with your order number and reason for return.
  2. We'll email you a prepaid return shipping label within 24 hours.
  3. Pack the item securely in its original packaging and attach the label.
  4. Drop it off at any authorised carrier location.
  5. Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund will be processed within 5–7 business days.

Conditions for Returns

  • Items must be returned within 30 days of the delivery date.
  • Products must be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging.
  • Include all accessories, manuals, and original tags.
  • Items marked as "Final Sale" are not eligible for return.

Damaged or Defective Items

If you received a damaged or defective item, please contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you.

Refund Timeline

Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we receive your return. Depending on your bank, it may take an additional 2–3 days to appear on your statement.
